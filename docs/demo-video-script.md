# Demo Video Script

Local businesses everywhere still need better websites. Most of them don’t have time to build one, and many don’t even know what a good site should look like. That’s the opportunity. This app gives you a simple way to find those businesses, create a website for them, and send a live preview with a personalized video that sells the site for you — plus a button for them to claim it. You don’t need a big team. You need a clear process. Here’s how it works inside the app.

---

## Dashboard

This is your dashboard. It’s the home base for the whole business. Here you see your leads, websites you’ve built, follow-ups that are due, recent activity, and what you should work on today. From this screen, the path is simple: find a lead, build the site, send the preview, and close.

---

## Find leads

From the dashboard, go to find leads. Here you search for local businesses by industry and location — anywhere you want to work. You can filter for businesses that need a stronger website, import a list, or add one business from a Google Maps link. Each lead is scored hot, warm, or cold, so you know who to contact first. Open a lead profile and you’ll see the business details: name, category, phone, address, hours, photos, and more. Pick one business — a store, clinic, contractor, salon — and you’re ready to build.

---

## Build the website

From the lead, create the website. With one click, the app builds a full website for that business: homepage, services, about, contact, reviews, and a design that fits. Open the site editor and you can change the copy, switch section layouts, add photos, bring in Google reviews, adjust colors and style, and add extra pages if you need them. You can also describe a change in plain language and let AI update the site for you. In a few minutes, you have a real website ready to show — not a rough mockup.

---

## Website features and AI sales assistant

The website is not empty. It includes the features a real business site needs: service pages, contact forms, Google reviews as testimonials, blog posts, multi-language options, and more. One standout feature is the AI sales assistant. Turn it on for the website. Visitors can chat with it, ask questions about the business, hear a natural voice reply, and leave their details when they’re ready. You can choose how the assistant looks, pick the voice, set the greeting, and place the launcher on the left or right. So even after you send the preview — or after the site goes live — the website keeps selling for you.

---

## Personalized sales video

Now create the video that sells this website to your lead. Open sales video. Generate or edit the script, choose a presenter, preview the voices, and create the video. This video is made for that exact website. It walks the business owner through their new site, explains why it matters, and invites them to open the live preview. The video does the pitching for you.

---

## Live preview and claim

Send the live preview. One link. The client opens a real website they can click through — their name, their services, their city, their reviews. The personalized video sells it for you. On the preview they can discuss the website, comment on a section, see pricing, or click Claim this website. When they claim it, you get the interest. From there you can send a proposal, get paid, publish the site on their domain, export the files, or transfer ownership. You can also keep hosting or maintenance as ongoing income.

---

## Close

That’s the flow. Start from your dashboard. Find leads anywhere. Build the website in one click. Add solid features — including an AI sales assistant. Create a personalized video that sells the site to the client. Send a live preview with a simple button to claim the website. A clear business. A clear process. A clear close. Open your dashboard and build your first preview today.
